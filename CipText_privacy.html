Privacy Policy

Welcome to CipText. We highly value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the app. By using CipText, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

1. App Overview

CipText is a personal privacy-focused application designed to create, store, and manage private text content. Its main features include private text notes, local encryption, and optional expiration-based automatic deletion.

CipText is designed with privacy-by-default principles and does not rely on network services to function.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

CipText does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of the following information:

1. Personal identification information such as name, phone number, email address, or ID numbers;
2. Any text content you create, edit, or store within the app;
3. Passwords, encryption keys, or decryption credentials;
4. Contacts, photos, messages, call logs, or other device private data;
5. Location data or device identifiers (such as IMEI, IDFA, or advertising identifiers).

3. Data Storage and Security

1. All data generated by CipText is stored locally on your device by default;
2. Encryption and decryption processes are performed entirely on the device;
3. The app developer cannot access, view, recover, or modify your content;
4. Data loss caused by device damage, system reset, app uninstallation, or forgotten passwords cannot be recovered by the developer.

4. Expiration and Auto-Deletion Feature

CipText allows users to set an expiration time for private text content. Once expired, the content will be automatically deleted locally on the device. This process does not involve any remote servers or data transmission.

5. Third-Party Services

CipText does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services. If any third-party services are introduced in future versions, users will be clearly informed and explicit consent will be obtained.

6. User Responsibilities

1. Users are responsible for securing their devices and access to the app;
2. Users are responsible for any data loss resulting from forgotten passwords or user actions;
3. Users must not use CipText for any illegal or unlawful activities.

7. Children’s Privacy

CipText is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under the age of majority, please use this app under the guidance and consent of a parent or legal guardian.
